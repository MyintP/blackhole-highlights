Pundit's Pick

Rams at Raiders · Week 7
Final score call
RAMS27RAIDERS24

Rams by three, but the Raiders cover +4.5. Las Vegas is 5-1 against the spread, and two of its three losses came by exactly three points. Total: 51, over 47.5.

  1. Catch the ball

    Seven drops sank the Bills game. Cousins has 14 touchdowns to 5 interceptions this season, so the passing game works when the hands do.

  2. Make Stafford uncomfortable

    Stafford leads the NFL with 1,794 passing yards, and the Raiders allow 371.7 yards a game (28th). Crosby has 2 sacks through six games. This is the week he needs more.

  3. Feed Jeanty early

    The run game ranks 30th at 79.2 yards a game. Even a modest ground game keeps Stafford on the sideline and takes pressure off Cousins.
