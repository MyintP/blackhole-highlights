EPA (expected points added) measures how much each play changed a team's expected points, given down, distance and field position. Success rate is the share of plays with positive EPA. CPOE is completion percentage above what's expected for the throws attempted. Tendencies rank 1 for the team that does it most. Formation, motion, play action, blitzers and box counts are charted by FTN; coverage shells (Cover 2, Cover 3 and so on) aren't published for free this season, so they aren't shown.
