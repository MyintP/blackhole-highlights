About Black Hole Highlights

A fan-run home for Las Vegas Raiders news and numbers, built alongside the Black Hole Highlights YouTube channel.

PurposeEditorial approachData sourcesContact

Why this site exists

Raiders fans shouldn't need ten tabs to follow their team. This site pulls the news, schedule, scores, injuries, betting lines and stats into one place and keeps them current automatically, then adds our own view of each game on top.

Black Hole Highlights is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NFL or the Las Vegas Raiders. Team names are used only to identify teams. We don't use team logos or NFL marks.

Editorial approach

Facts and opinions are kept apart:

  • Facts update automatically. Scores, schedule, standings, stats, injuries, odds and headlines come straight from the sources below. We never invent them, and the only figures entered by hand are the Weeks 1-6 betting records described under Data sources.
  • Opinions are ours and labelled. The Pundit's Pick, keys to the game and milestones are written by Black Hole Highlights and shown only for the game they're about.
  • Every story links to its publisher. We show the headline and the publisher's own short summary, never the full article, and we don't republish publisher photos.

Each story carries a label so you know who is talking:

Official
Published by the Raiders on Raiders.com.
Reporting
News from an established outlet such as ESPN or the Las Vegas Review-Journal.
Opinion
Fan analysis and opinion, such as Silver & Black Pride and RaidersBeat.
Rumor
Speculation or reports the team hasn't confirmed, whoever published them. Treat these with care.

When several outlets cover the same story, it appears once with links to the others under "Also covered by".

Betting lines are shown for information and entertainment only. You must be 21 or older to bet. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Data sources and update schedule

  • Scores, schedule, standings, team and player stats, injuries, roster: ESPN's public data feeds, checked every hour, and every 10 minutes during a game.
  • Advanced stats, tendencies, player profiles and play-by-play breakdowns: nflverse (CC BY 4.0): NFL play-by-play with the nflfastR EPA and win-probability models, FTN charting (formation, motion, play action, blitzers, box counts), Pro Football Reference charting (coverage, pressures, missed tackles, drops, yards after contact) and snap counts. Checked twice a day; nflverse usually updates the morning after each game. Coverage shells such as Cover 2 aren't in any free 2026 source, so the site doesn't show them.
  • Betting lines: DraftKings lines published by ESPN, captured every hour from 12 hours to 1 hour before kickoff (around game time the update job re-runs itself on that schedule, because GitHub's own timer often runs hours late). The last capture is the closing line used to grade results against the spread.
  • News: the public news feeds of Raiders.com, Las Vegas Review-Journal, ESPN, Silver & Black Pride and RaidersBeat, checked every hour. Stories are kept for 180 days.
  • Podcasts: the Raiders.com audio feed. Videos: the Black Hole Highlights YouTube channel.
  • Records against the spread for Weeks 1-6 of 2026 come from public betting reports. Later weeks are graded from our own closing-line captures.

If a source doesn't respond, the site keeps showing its last good data and says so in a notice at the top of the page. To tell you when data was last checked, your browser asks GitHub's public API when the hourly update last ran. No personal data is sent, and the site uses no cookies, tracking or analytics.

Contact and corrections

Spotted a mistake or have a story tip? Leave a comment on any video on the Black Hole Highlights YouTube channel, or open an issue on the site's GitHub page. Data errors are usually fixed at the source within the hour; we correct our own writing as soon as we hear about it.
